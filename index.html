      Guidelines reference

      Summary of the EDM Template guideline 2026 used by every check on this page.

      Mandatory design elements

      • Background off-white #fffffb
      • Fonts Helvetica Neue / Helvetica / Arial (body) and Georgia (titles)
      • Body text dark grey #5f5f5f; titles dark brown #5e4501
      • Dividers grey #b4adad
      • Buttons, #instantbenefits and Sign up in the hotel's brand color
      • Every image has href, alt text and title text

      Six sections

      • Hotel logo: horizontal international logo, alt and title "Hotel Logo", links to the hotel landing page, divider below
      • Main offer: HD hero, room package recommended, alt = promotion detail, title = promotion name, button "Book Now", divider below
      • Other offers: HD images linked like their button, description up to 160 characters, divider below the last offer
      • About SBEC: managed by group; adjust the two colors only, Sign up links to swiss-belhotel.com
      • Footer banner: latest banner from Group Marcomm; alt and title = program name
      • Footer email: change only social links (reservation email, Facebook, Instagram), address and local phone

      Buttons and links

      • Room package: Book Now → hotel website
      • F&B package: Reserve → reservation contact
      • Social event: Reserve → reservation contact
      • Meeting package: Reserve → reservation contact

      Whizzmailer settings

      • Internal name SBSE - 06 Jun 2026
      • UTM campaign name whizz-edm-SBSE-06Jun2026
      • Campaign source edm, medium whizzmailer: do not modify
      • Stop sending at most 5 days after the start
      • From name: complete hotel name; reply-to: hotel email
      • Account time zone is Jakarta (GMT+7); allow 20–30 minutes sending delay
      • One to two EDMs per hotel per month; a delivered template cannot be resent
      • Tickets and their screenshots are deleted automatically 3 months and 1 day after they are created. Keep your own copy if you need it longer.
